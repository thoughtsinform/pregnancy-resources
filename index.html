Take a breath.

Finding out you're pregnant when you didn't plan to can make the whole world go quiet.

Maybe you're scared. Maybe you haven't told anyone yet. Maybe you feel pulled in five directions at once and don't know which voice to trust. Whatever you're carrying right now, you don't have to sort it out by yourself.

Pregnancy Resources is a neutral, welcoming space to slow down and think it all through before anything is decided. We'll listen to your questions, give you clear information, and simply be there for you. You set the pace. We'll be right here.

What we offer.

  • Pregnancy confirmation

    Not sure yet? Start here. Our trained team will help you confirm your pregnancy and understand exactly where things stand.

  • An honest options conversation

    Clear, truthful information about the expected benefits and risks of every option in front of you, so nothing about your future feels like a guess.

  • Decision coaching

    Room to process before you choose. We help you sort through what matters most to you, and we never rush you.

  • Support after you decide

    Whatever path you take, you won't walk it alone. Care, conversation, and follow-up are here for you on the other side.

  • Education

    Practical, encouraging guidance for the road ahead, from people who genuinely want to see you and your family thrive.

  • Community connections

    Brevard County has good help available. We know it well, and we'll walk you to the right doors for what you need next.

Every service is free. Walk in Wednesday or Thursday from 1pm to 4pm, or request a visit ahead of time.

We don't profit from your choice.

Every service we offer is free. We don't perform or refer for abortions, we don't provide ongoing prenatal care, and we aren't an adoption agency.

That means nothing we do depends on what you decide. Our only hope is that you walk out informed, supported, and deeply cared for.

Stronger than this moment.

Right now it might feel impossible. For more than forty years we've sat with women who felt exactly that way, and we've watched them find their footing, their voice, and their people.

There is a whole community here ready to hold you up. And the little life quietly growing inside you is worth every bit of care we can give you both.

You can do this. We'll do it together.

Questions you might have.

Does it really cost nothing?

Yes. Every service is free, made possible by generous community support. You will never receive a bill from us.

Is my visit confidential?

Yes. Your visit and your conversation stay private. We follow HIPAA privacy standards, and we never sell, rent, or share your personal information for marketing purposes.

Will anyone pressure me?

No. This is your life and your decision. We give you honest information, listen to what matters to you, and let you move at your own pace.

Do you provide abortions?

We don't perform or refer for abortions. We do provide honest information about the benefits and risks of all options, including all types of abortion, so you can decide with the full picture in front of you.

I've already decided. Can I still come?

Absolutely. Whether you're unsure or you've already made a choice, you're welcome here. Support is available on the other side of every decision, including post-abortion support.

Do I need an appointment?

Walk-ins are welcome Wednesdays and Thursdays from 1pm to 4pm. Requesting a visit ahead of time helps you avoid a wait. Last appointment is one hour before closing.

Can I bring someone with me?

Of course. A partner, parent, or trusted friend is welcome to come along. Many people find it helps to not walk in alone.
